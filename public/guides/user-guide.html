Marketing Page And Pricing

Use the public home page to explain Dispatcher365, show the 14-day card-required trial, and send customers to signup or shipment tracking.

  1. Open the home page.
  2. Review pricing and the fees menu.
  3. Select Start 14-Day Trial when the customer is ready to set up billing.

Company Signup

A company signs up first. The workspace stays pending until super admin approval and billing are active.

  1. Enter company name and fleet size.
  2. Choose plan and active driver count.
  3. Create the first admin user.
  4. Use Stripe trial setup to add the card.

Public Shipment Tracking

Customers can track loads without logging into the dispatcher portal.

  1. Open Shipment Tracking.
  2. Enter the load or tracking ID.
  3. Review status history, stops, and approved documents.

Admin Home

Admins manage company setup, users, drivers, equipment, assignments, billing status, and reports.

  1. Log in as admin or super admin.
  2. Use the dashboard cards to open setup areas.
  3. Watch company approval and billing status before users log in.

Company Approval And Billing

Super admin approves companies and can see whether billing is active or blocking access.

  1. Open Company Setup.
  2. Approve pending companies.
  3. Use Mark Paid only when billing has been verified.

Dispatch Board And Create Load

Dispatchers create loads, add stops, assign drivers and compatible equipment, and monitor unassigned loads.

  1. Open Dispatch / Loads.
  2. Use saved customer/location fields to reduce typing.
  3. Assign driver and vehicle.
  4. Use the board to monitor today, upcoming, unassigned, and in-transit work.

Driver Check-In And Inspection

Drivers must complete a vehicle inspection before becoming ready for work or updating assigned loads.

  1. Driver logs in on mobile.
  2. Open Check-In.
  3. Submit pre-trip inspection.
  4. Start shift after the inspection unlocks readiness.

Driver Assigned Work

Drivers update pickup, transit, delivery, BOL/POD, exceptions, and signatures from the mobile assigned work page.

  1. Open Assigned Work.
  2. Use one-tap load status buttons.
  3. Upload required proof documents.
  4. Capture signature when needed.

Customer Tracking And Visibility

Dispatch can copy public tracking links and control which proof documents customers can see.

  1. Open Customer Tracking.
  2. Search customer or load.
  3. Copy update text or tracking links.
  4. Choose public BOL/POD visibility on the load card.

Reports, Settings, And Support

Reports summarize load activity, inspections, defects, documents, and driver activity. Settings show company configuration, notifications, and backup guidance.

  1. Open Reports for operational summaries.
  2. Open Settings to confirm setup status.
  3. Use Bug Reports when staff or drivers need to report an app issue.

Important Notes

Each company keeps its own company-scoped data. If billing has a payment issue, portal access can be blocked. Super admin approval and active billing are both required.
