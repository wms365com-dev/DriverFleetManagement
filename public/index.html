Fleet dispatch software FAQ

Built for carriers that need dispatch, driver updates, and customer visibility without a complicated rollout.

What is Dispatcher365?

Dispatcher365 is web-based dispatch software for managing loads, drivers, inspections, equipment, saved customer locations, BOL/POD uploads, and public shipment tracking links.

Can customers track their loads?

Yes. Dispatchers can copy a public tracking link for each load so customers can see shipment status, pickup and delivery details, status history, and approved proof documents.

Does it work on mobile?

Drivers get mobile-friendly pages for assigned work, check-ins, inspections, issue reports, GPS permission, and proof-of-delivery uploads from the field.

Who is it for?

Dispatcher365 is designed for small and growing trucking, courier, delivery, and private fleet operators that need a simple operations system for dispatchers, admins, and drivers.

Can dispatchers save addresses?

Yes. Customer locations, operating hours, contact names, phone numbers, dock notes, and site instructions can be saved for faster load entry.

Does it replace Samsara or Motive?

Dispatcher365 focuses on dispatch workflow, driver check-ins, inspections, documents, and customer tracking. Hardware ELD, dashcam, and advanced telematics integrations can be added later.
